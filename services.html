FAQ

Service letter.

Four answers on the jacket before the form. Shed and plant, bank templates, rush, and what sits in the industrial fee.

Service letter SPV-FAQ · 4 answers Before instruct
01Can two lines sit on one instruction?

Shed and plant should be two opinions. A house and a shop on the same plot can be one file if the purpose is one.

02Do you match a bank’s template?

Yes, on panel files. The fee stays ours; the photograph order becomes theirs.

03Is rush guaranteed?

Rush is a surcharge and a capacity question. We do not sell a fake 24-hour stamp.

04Is plant in the industrial fee?

No. The industrial line is the shed and the land. Plant is a separate instruction, quoted apart, GST extra.

Open one answer at a time. The clock still runs from the paid slot.
