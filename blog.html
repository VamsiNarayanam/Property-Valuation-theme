Terms

Glossary of valuation words.

Six words as they sit on the jacket. Not a statute, and not a brochure.

Glossary SPV-GLS · 6 terms On the cover
01

Basis

Market value
Estimated exchange price at the valuation date, stated purpose, stated assumptions.
02

Panel

Force-sale value
A lower figure used on some bank panels. Never blended into market value.
03

Cover

Desktop review
Opinion without a full site survey. Must be labelled on the cover.
04

Archive

Working papers
Comps, cost workings, income tests. Numbered with the report revision.
05

Area

Carpet / built-up
We state which area we used. Brochure RERA carpet is not always the measured carpet.
06

Clock

TAT
Turnaround from paid inspection slot to signed PDF, in calendar days.

Words as used on the jacket. They are not a legal dictionary.
