FAQ

Five answers before you instruct.

Fees, remote surveys, listing prices, the Fort desk, and what is dummy on this demo.

01Is the starting fee the whole bill?

No. ₹45,000 is the public starting line for a standard Bengaluru residential file. GST is extra. Travel outside the urban map, rush slots, drone roofs, and plant & machinery are surcharges.

02Do you accept remote inspections?

Yes, where a video walk-through, a measuring tape in frame, and a complete photograph set can support the stated purpose. The cover will say remote. Some bank panels still require attendance.

03Can you value a property for a listing price?

We file opinions of value for stated purposes (sale, mortgage, insurance, litigation, IBC-related work in demo form). We do not write marketing copy or “dream home” narratives.

04What does Fort, Mumbai do?

Viewing desk for bank-panel files and Mumbai inspections. Survey management remains in Bengaluru. Hours are on Contact.

05Are names and GSTIN on this site real?

No. This is a ThemeForest demo. GSTIN, IBBI, RERA panel numbers, banks, and clients are dummy.
